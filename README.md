## 📖 Why this list?

There are hundreds of AI tools launched every week. Most are noise. This list is **what I would actually recommend** to a friend who asked *"what should I be using?"* — covering 14 real-world workflows from coding to research to video.

Every entry has been used by me or rigorously vetted. No affiliate links. No paid placements. **If a tool gets worse or shuts down, it gets removed.**

> 🛎️ **What changed since the 2025 edition:** Sora discontinued (April 2026), Galileo AI became Google Stitch, Tome killed its slides product, Claude Code went mainstream, and a new wave of vibe-coding tools (Lovable, v0, Bolt) reshaped how people build apps.

## 🧭 How to choose

A useful framework for picking a tool from this list:

1. **What problem are you actually trying to solve?** Don't pick a category — pick a verb. *"Summarize a meeting"* not *"a productivity tool."*
2. **Where does the AI need to live?** In your IDE? Browser? Terminal? Phone? Your existing tools?
3. **Privacy & data sensitivity?** If you're touching customer data or proprietary code, prefer local (Ollama, LM Studio) or enterprise tiers with no-training guarantees.
4. **Free tier first.** Almost every tool here has a free tier or trial. Try before you pay.
5. **One tool per workflow.** The 2026 trend isn't *more* tools — it's three or four that quietly do real work.
